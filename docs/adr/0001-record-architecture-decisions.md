# 1. Record architecture decisions

Status: accepted (2026-11-01)

## Context

This repo is an experiment meant to inform a later, larger build. The reasons behind decisions are the most valuable output and the easiest to lose.

## Decision

Record each significant decision as a short ADR in `docs/adr/`, numbered in order, with Context, Decision and Consequences. An accepted ADR is never edited; a later ADR supersedes it.

## Consequences

A small writing cost per decision, in exchange for an auditable history that `LESSONS.md` can draw on.
