## General review, 2026-11-10

Findings of a whole-codebase review at commit `d3104a3`. "Reproduced" means the behaviour was triggered and observed; "read" means it comes from reading the code and was not run. Update a row's status as it moves, and remove the row when it is closed. At that commit `npm run check` passed (1,156 tests) and `npm run test:web` passed (61 tests). The review did not go through the validator's individual rules, most of the forked page, `qa-scenarios.md` or `review-checklist.md`.

Three findings are rules of the game and need the owner's ruling, so they are in [parked-product-questions.md](parked-product-questions.md) as CQ-3 to CQ-5: an absent host, a tie-break that lowers a late joiner's rank, and last-moment answers.
